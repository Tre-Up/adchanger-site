4.2How a Verified minute is counted

  • A minute counts once the whole advertisement has been on screen for 60 seconds while your Mac is awake and in use.
  • If you are away from your Mac for 8 minutes, the count pauses. The count also pauses if the bar is covered or has no room in the menu bar.
  • If your Mac locks, sleeps or shuts down, or you pause, snooze or stop the bar, the unfinished minute does not count.
  • Our server checks every minute and accepts each minute only once. The server’s count is the count on which payment is based.
