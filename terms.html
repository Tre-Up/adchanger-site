Our privacy notice explains how we handle your data.

Early access

AdChanger is still being built and the download is not public yet. Features may change, pause or not be switched on yet, including payouts. We will keep these terms up to date as that happens. Please report anything that looks wrong.

Who can use AdChanger

  • You must be 13 or older. Tell us your real age band; the rules for under-18s depend on it.

  • Anyone with a Mac, in any country, can earn. An ad only shows in the countries its advertiser chose, so what is on offer depends on where you are.

  • You must be 18 or older to advertise, and you must be allowed to act for the business you advertise.

  • One account per person. Keep access to your email and sign-in safe; you are responsible for what happens in your account.

For people who show ads (earners)

The bar

MacAd shows one ad at a time in your menu bar. You can pause, snooze or stop it at any time. Sometimes the bar shows AdChanger's own message instead of a paid ad, for example when no ad fits you well enough or during a short break between paid stretches. Those minutes earn nothing.

How a minute is counted

  • A minute counts after the whole ad has been on screen for 60 seconds while your Mac is awake and in use.

  • If you are away for 8 minutes, the clock waits. If the bar is covered or has no room in the menu bar, the clock waits too.

  • If your Mac locks, sleeps or shuts down, or you pause, snooze or stop the bar, the unfinished minute does not count.

  • Our server checks every minute and accepts each one once. Its count is the one that pays.

What you earn

Advertisers pay £4, €5 or $6 per 1,000 verified minutes, depending on their currency. You keep 65% of each minute you show; AdChanger keeps 35%. If you joined with someone's affiliate code, you keep 70%, the code's owner gets 5% and AdChanger keeps 25%. Shares are rounded down to the smallest unit we track.

  • One person is counted for at most 1,000 minutes of the same ad a day.

  • Only one of your Macs earns at a time.

  • You never earn from your own business's ads.

  • We do not promise any amount. What you earn depends on the ads running where you are and how long your Mac is in use.

Getting paid

  • Each minute's earnings wait 3 days, so we can check for invalid activity. Then they become available.

  • You can withdraw available earnings from £10, €10 or $10 upwards, one withdrawal at a time.

  • Payouts go through Whop. You link a Whop account and Whop checks your ID once. From Whop you send the money to your bank or card on Whop's page.

  • Whop's own terms and fees apply to that step. Whop's fees come out of the amount you send.

  • You are responsible for any tax on what you earn.

Affiliate codes

You can add someone's affiliate code once. While it is active, you keep 70% and they get 5% of your minutes. If you remove it, you still keep 70%, the 5% goes to AdChanger, and you cannot add a code again. The price advertisers pay never changes.

Swapping earnings for a gift code

Where a brand offers it, adults can swap part of their available earnings for that brand's gift code, worth more than they give. The amount leaves your balance at once. The code is the brand's, can only be used with that brand and follows its terms.

Invalid activity

We only pay for real people really using their Mac. You must not:

  • use scripts, bots, virtual machines or anything else to fake activity;

  • change the app, its clock or its messages to the server, or replay minutes;

  • run several accounts, or share one account across people;

  • click your own ads, or ads you have no interest in, or ask others to;

  • hide where you are to get ads for another country.

If we see signs of this, minutes may not count, earnings may be held while we check, money paid for invalid minutes may be taken back, and the account may be closed. Where we can, we will tell you why and let you explain.

For businesses that run ads (advertisers)

Your business

You set your business's billing country once, before your first payment. It fixes your currency: pounds in the UK, euros in the EU, dollars everywhere else. Prices are £4, €5 or $6 per 1,000 verified minutes in those currencies.

Your ad

  • You are responsible for your ad, its link and the page it opens. You must have the right to use every word, logo and image in it, and what it says must be true.

  • The ad is a still image (no animation) at the size the app gives you, with a secure https:// link.

  • A person reviews every ad, its link and the page it opens before it runs. We may approve it, ask for changes or reject it, and we tell you why.

  • If you change an approved ad, the change is reviewed again before it runs.

  • We may stop an ad that is running if it breaks these terms or the law.

We do not accept ads for:

  • gambling or betting;

  • adult sexual content or services;

  • prescription medicines;

  • politics or elections;

  • speculative crypto or token investments.

Ads for things only adults may buy, such as alcohol or dating, are never shown to anyone under 18. Sensitive subjects get a closer look in review.

Paying

  • You add money through Whop before a campaign runs. Whop takes the card; we never see it. Each payment is between 10 and 10,000 in your currency.

  • The money becomes your credit. A campaign's budget is set aside from it in full before the campaign starts.

  • The budget is spent one verified minute at a time. When it runs out, the bar stops. You are never charged more than your budget.

  • You pay nothing for unfinished minutes, while an earner has paused, snoozed or stopped the bar, or while your campaign is not running.

  • If a minute is shown past your budget by our mistake, you do not pay for it.

Unused budget and refunds

Budget you have not spent goes back to your credit for your next campaign. We do not pay unused credit back in cash. The one exception: if we reject your ad and not one minute of it ran, we refund that payment. This does not affect any right you have by law.

Results

You see totals: verified minutes, spend, people who saw, noticed and clicked your ad, reached your page and became customers. Groups of fewer than 5 people are merged, so no one can be singled out. You never get a list of people. A sale counts for your ad when it happens within 7 days of a click on it. If your ad carries a discount code, you must honour it. If you report sales to us from your website or shop, you are responsible for telling your visitors in your own privacy notice.

Gift-code offers

You may offer earners your own gift codes in exchange for part of their earnings. The amount an earner swaps goes to your credit for future campaigns. You must honour every code you give us.
