Privacy notice

AdChanger is built to know as little about you as it can. This page says exactly what it does collect, why, who handles it and what you can do about it.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

The short version

  • The app never sees your screen, what you type, your microphone or camera, what is inside your apps or your browsing history.

  • To count a minute it only checks that the ad is on screen and that your Mac is awake and in use.

  • Your answers about yourself are optional. They are stored as fixed choices; words you type yourself stay on your Mac.

  • Advertisers only ever see totals. They never get your name, your email or a list of people.

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with ad networks or data brokers.

  • This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Who we are

AdChanger is run by its founder, Yiğit Ali Kurt. It is not a registered company yet. He decides how your data is used, so for data protection law he is responsible for it. Write to yigit@adchanger.net about anything on this page.

This notice covers the AdChanger Mac app (including MacAd, the ad in the menu bar) and this website, adchanger.net. It applies to people who show ads and earn (earners) and to businesses that run ads (advertisers).

What the app never collects

  • Your screen, screenshots or screen recordings. The app asks for no screen-recording permission.

  • Your keystrokes or anything you type in other apps. The app asks for no Accessibility permission.

  • Your microphone or camera.

  • What is inside your apps, documents or messages.

  • Your browsing or search history.

  • Your contacts, your exact location or a list of the apps you have installed.

  • Your clipboard. When you open a bar that comes with a discount code, the app copies the code for you; it never reads what is on your clipboard.

  • Your health, medicines, pregnancy, mental health, religion, politics or sexuality. We never ask about these.

What we collect, and why

For each kind of data we say why we need it and the legal reason that lets us use it under the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR.

Your account

Your email address. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, also the basic profile those services pass on, such as your name. Your age band (13 to 15, 16 or 17, or 18 or over), the date you joined and your country. We work out your country from the connection your Mac uses or, if that is not available, from your Mac's region setting. We do not store your internet address in our database.

Why: to run your account, keep your earnings safe and follow the age rules below. Legal reason: to provide the service you asked for (contract).

Your Mac

A random ID for each Mac you use AdChanger on, the app version and the macOS version. Nothing else about your hardware.

Why: to know which Mac is earning (one Mac per person earns at a time) and to fix problems. Legal reason: contract, and our legitimate interest in preventing fraud.

Minutes and the bar

For each minute an ad runs: which ad it was, when the minute started and finished, and how many times your pointer rested on the bar (we call this “noticed”). We also record when you start, pause, snooze or stop the bar, when you open its window, when the bar is covered or has no room, how long the pointer rested on it, and taps such as “Not for me”.

Why: to count verified minutes, pay you, charge advertisers correctly, spot fake activity and give advertisers honest totals. Legal reason: contract, and our legitimate interests in preventing fraud and measuring results.

Clicks

When you click a bar, we record the click and give it a random ID. The advertiser's link then opens with that ID added (as mac=) so a sale can be matched to the bar. What happens on the advertiser's own website is covered by their privacy notice.

Why: so advertisers pay for results they can check, and so we can show you the ads you clicked. Legal reason: contract and legitimate interests.

Your answers (optional)

What you do on your Mac, your interests, what you plan to buy, life moments such as moving house, and subjects you never want ads about. These are sent as fixed choices from our list. Words you type in your own answers stay on your Mac. Plans count less after 60 days and stop counting after 120.

Why: to show you ads that fit you. Legal reason: your consent. Answering is optional, and you can change or clear your answers in Settings at any time.

The app in front (the moment)

On your Mac, the app checks which app you are using right now, for example a coding tool or a design tool, so it can pick a bar that fits. Which app it is never leaves your Mac. Our server only learns two yes-or-no answers: whether the moment was used to choose the bar, and whether the chosen bar fitted it. One choice in five leaves the moment out on purpose, so we can measure whether it helps.

Legal reason: our legitimate interest in showing relevant ads. You can switch it off in Settings under “Match the bar to what I'm doing”.

What the app learns on your Mac

While your answers are in use, the app also learns from what you do with its own bar: which bars you rest on, open, click or pass over. This stays on your Mac, counts half as much every 30 days and is forgotten when you clear your answers. It never looks at anything outside AdChanger's own bar.

Earnings and payouts

What you earned each minute, what is waiting, what is available, your withdrawals and the ID of your Whop account. If you use an affiliate code, which code and when. We never see or store your bank or card details: Whop holds them.

Why: to pay you the right amount, once. Legal reason: contract, and our legal duty to keep financial records.

Advertisers

The business name, website and billing country; who in the team can do what; campaigns, bar images, links, discount codes, budgets and review decisions; and each payment's amount and Whop reference. Card details go to Whop, never to us. If you offer gift codes to earners, we keep those codes secret until an earner swaps for one.

Legal reason: contract, and our legal duty to keep financial records.

Bug reports and emails

If you send a bug report from the app, we get what you write and any screenshots or videos you choose to attach. If you email us, we get your message and email address. Legal reason: our legitimate interest in fixing problems and answering you.

What advertisers see

  • Totals: verified minutes, spend, how many people saw, noticed and clicked the bar, reached their page and became customers.

  • Results by place and time are grouped. A group with fewer than 5 people is merged into “other”, so no one is singled out.

  • Audience sizes while they plan a campaign are rounded, and hidden below that same floor.

  • Never your name, email, Mac, answers or a list of people.

Who else handles your data

We use a few services to run AdChanger. Each only gets what it needs for its job.

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in, file storage and server code. Our database is in London.

  • Whop: advertiser card payments, earner payouts and the ID check Whop needs before it pays out. Whop also has its own privacy policy for what you give it directly.

  • Google and GitHub: only if you choose to sign in with them.

  • Google Workspace: our email, including messages you send to us.

  • GitHub (GitHub Pages): hosts this website.

We choose which ads you see in our own database and on your Mac. No outside company receives your answers. If we ever use an outside tool to improve how ads are chosen, it will get only data with no names, emails, typed words, internet addresses or payment details.

Data outside the UK

Our database is in the UK. Some of the services above, such as Whop, Google and GitHub, are based in the United States and may handle data there. When data leaves the UK or the European Economic Area, we rely on the safeguards these providers offer for such transfers. Write to us if you want to know more.

How long we keep it

  • Your answers stay until you change or clear them, or close your account. Plans stop counting after 120 days.

  • What the app learns on your Mac counts half as much every 30 days and is forgotten when you clear your answers.

  • Minute, click, payment and earnings records are never edited once written, so the money always adds up. We keep them after an account closes, because we need them for accounts and tax.

  • When you close your account, we delete your answers and anything we worked out from them, disconnect your Macs and remove your name and city. Your email and financial records stay with the closed account.

  • We have not yet set fixed periods after which closed accounts are deleted in full. We will add them to this page.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR you can ask us to: show you the data we hold about you and give you a copy; correct it; delete it; stop or limit using it; and move it to another service. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Where we rely on legitimate interests, you can object.

To use any of these rights, write to yigit@adchanger.net from the email address on your account. We will answer within one month. Some things you can do yourself in the app: change or clear your answers, switch off matching to the app in front, and pause, snooze or stop the bar.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) in the UK, or to the data protection authority where you live in the EU. We would like the chance to put it right first.

Children and young people

  • You must be 13 or older to use AdChanger. If you say you are under 13, the app stops and stores nothing.

  • People aged 13 to 17 can earn. They never see ads for alcohol, gambling, tobacco, dating or adult content.

  • In the UK and Europe, under-18s start with their answers switched off. They see everyday ads unless they choose to switch answers on.

  • You must be 18 or older to advertise or to swap earnings for a brand's gift code.

  • Whether and how a payout reaches someone under 18 depends on Whop's rules.

How we keep it safe

Each person can read only their own records, and each advertiser only their own business's. Money, minutes and clicks can only be written by our server, never by the app directly. We hold no passwords: you sign in with an email link, Google or GitHub. The advertiser pages in the app open only after Touch ID or your Mac's password. Staff actions are logged.

Cookies and storage on this website

This website sets no cookies, uses no local storage and runs no analytics or tracking. Like any web host, GitHub sees your internet address and browser details when it sends you a page. The download and email buttons open your own email app; nothing is sent until you send it.

Changes to this notice

AdChanger is in early access and will change. When this notice changes, we update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your data, we will tell you in the app or by email before it applies.
