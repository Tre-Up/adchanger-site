Questions

Straight answers.

What is MacAd?
One small bar in the Mac menu bar, next to the clock, that shows your brand to people who chose to see ads and are paid for it. You build the bar, pick who sees it and pay only for verified minutes. MacAd is a product by AdChanger: you set it up in the AdChanger app.
What is a verified minute?
A minute counts only after the ad has been on screen for 60 seconds while you are actually using your Mac. Step away and the clock waits. Close the lid and it starts over. Advertisers pay for verified minutes and nothing else.
Who sees my bar?
People whose Mac fits your campaign: the countries and cities you pick, what they do on their Mac, what they are into and what they plan to buy, in words they chose themselves. On top, the moment: the app in front of them right now. If nothing fits well enough, no paid bar shows, so you never pay for filler.
What do advertisers see in their results?
Verified minutes and spend, then how many people saw the bar, clicked it, reached your page and became customers. Results by place never single anyone out: places with fewer than 5 people are grouped together. You set a hard budget limit, and when it is used up the bar stops.
How do I pay?
Through Whop, for now (Stripe, Wise and PayPal are coming soon). Whop takes the card, and AdChanger never sees it. You add your budget before a campaign runs; it is spent minute by minute, and whatever isn’t spent stays your credit.
Is every ad allowed?
A person reviews every bar, its link and the page it opens before it runs. Gambling, adult content and prescription drugs are never shown, and sensitive subjects get a closer look.

Questions

Straight answers.

What is AdChanger?
AdChanger is a Mac app that puts one small, quiet ad in your menu bar and pays you for every minute it is seen. Businesses use the same app to build the ad, choose who sees it and pay only for verified minutes. Its first product is MacAd, the ad in the menu bar.
How much can I earn?
An advertiser pays £4 for 1,000 verified minutes (€5 or $6 in euros and dollars), and you keep 65% of that: £2.60 per 1,000 minutes. With someone’s affiliate code you keep 70%, they get 5% and AdChanger keeps 25%. An 8-hour day with an ad running the whole time comes to about £1.25. What you really earn depends on the ads running for your country and how long your Mac is awake and in use, so we promise no fixed number.
Is AdChanger safe?
It is built to see as little as possible. To count a minute, MacAd only checks whether your Mac is awake and in use. It never sees your screen, what you type, your microphone or camera, what is inside your apps or your browsing history, and it needs no Accessibility or screen-recording permission. You can pause, snooze or stop it at any time, and a person reviews every ad before it runs.
How do I get paid?
Through Whop, for now (Stripe, Wise and PayPal are coming soon). Each minute’s earnings wait 3 days, then you can withdraw from £10, as often as you like. Whop checks your ID once and keeps your bank details; AdChanger never sees them.
What is a verified minute?
A minute counts only after the ad has been on screen for 60 seconds while you are actually using your Mac. Step away and the clock waits. Close the lid and it starts over. Advertisers pay for verified minutes and nothing else.
Who can use it, and where?
Anyone with a Mac, in any country, can earn; an ad only shows in the countries its advertiser picked. Advertisers can run ads anywhere in the world. The UK is our first market.
Can I turn it off?
Always. Pause it, snooze it, or stop it from the menu bar. You never need to uninstall anything.
When can I download it?
Soon. The Mac app is being built now, and the download will be right here the day it’s ready.
